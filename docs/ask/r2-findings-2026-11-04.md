the blind by-kind report (`docs/ask/r2-heldout-1-by-kind.md`) and the day's runs. Everything here is on branch

## 2. What the misses are

**Dev (69 misses of 158), with grades:**

| Kind | n | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Answer in the prompt, reply not grounded (wrong passage cited, a number misread, a two-part question half answered, an adjacent fact) | 25 | 36% |
| Answer in the prompt, model declined | 7 | 10% |
| Declined by the ranking gate before the model ran | 8 | 12% |
| Found by a search, not among the 4 | 18 | 26% |
| Never found by either search | 11 | 16% |

So on dev the model owns 46% of the misses and retrieval 54%. Classics: 10 of its 25 misses had the answer in the
prompt. On held-out (blind, by kind) retrieval was 25 of 42 and the model 17 of 42.

**Inside retrieval, by kind** (dev; held-out and tuning agree):
- *Found, not chosen* is the big one, not *never found*: counting a hit anywhere in either search's list, only 15
  of 148 dev answers are never found; 43 are found and not chosen. Classics carries 20 of those 43.
- *The right source, the wrong chunk*: in 27 dev misses (19 tuning, 30 held-out) the right book or article was among
  the 4 but another chunk of it. All 79 such chunks came from the dense list, across 67 different chapters, and the
  answer's chunk scores **lower** lexically than the chosen ones in 15 of 16: the other chapters use the question's
  words, the answer's chapter doesn't. No reweighting fixes that.
- *Wikipedia about the work beats the work*: in the 20 classics found-not-chosen misses, `wikipedia_en_top` filled
  36 of the 80 slots. Scripture questions ("who got swallowed by a giant fish") got four encyclopedia passages.
- *Fiction without a cue word*: questions whose answer is in a novel, with no literary word in them ("novel",
  "character", "wrote"…), missed 13 of 20 on dev and 8 of 9 on held-out; with a cue word 4 of 9 and 4 of 8.
- *Plot descriptions with no title* ("the story with the headless horseman"): 5 of 42 held-out misses, several on
  dev; the keywords become plot nouns that match unrelated articles.
- *The Classics notes* (one-page cards for the 50 most-read works, built for exactly these questions) are found and
  not chosen: among the hits for 13 of 31 held-out classics questions and chosen for 2; on dev a note for the right
  work was among the hits for 7 of 26 classics misses, holding the answer's words for 5. The dev set deliberately
  spans 42 books, mostly outside the 50.
- *Spanish passages on English questions*: took a slot on 17 of 202 tuning, 10 of 171 held-out, 11 of 209 dev
  questions. Fixed today (`ResearchPipeline` drops passages whose language isn't the question's).
- *The gate* declines 8 dev answerables before the model (6 with the passage found by a search), but lowering it
  recovers none of them by replay (their passage isn't in the top 4 ungated either) and costs 11 declines. It also
  does most of the declining: 56 of tuning's 71 declines, 26 of held-out's 44, 25 of dev's 51 come from it.

**Multi-step**: 5 of 19 held-out, 13 of 20 tuning (after the date work). With both facts in the prompt the model
often declines ("the passages don't give the computed value") or skips the arithmetic.

## 4. What's left, by kind

Retrieval (bounded, cheap, by kind):
1. **A reserved slot for a Classics note of the named work** when one is among the hits (mirror `Entities.reserve`).
   Ceiling: about 5 of 26 classics misses on dev, 8 of 14 found-not-chosen classics on held-out.
2. **A second look inside the found source**: when the 4 concentrate on one book or article, search within it for
   the answer's chunk by a different route than the question's words (the dense neighbours of the chosen chunks, the
   rewrite's alternates, or the chapter whose heading matches). Addresses the 27-per-set "right source, wrong chunk"
   kind; costs a search step, not a weight.
3. **Plot descriptions with no title**: route them to the notes and to Wikipedia's article about the work, or ask the
   model for the title first (one short extra turn).

Model (the half retrieval can't reach):
4. **A self-check pass**: after the answer, the model checks each cited claim against its passage and withdraws or
   corrects; replayable over recorded prompts on the emulator (`answerers.py`), a few seconds a turn on the phone.
5. **Multi-step arithmetic in code** beyond dates (counts, ages, differences), since the model won't do it.
6. **The LoRA**, when LiteRT-LM loads adapters on the GPU bundle, or a merged model if its halved decode speed is
   acceptable on big phones only.
7. **A larger model on bigger phones** (the E4B catalog entry is still there, `evalPassed: false`).

Not levers: more passages (tried), reweighting (tried five ways), lowering the gate (tried).

## 5. How we work now

- **Three sets, three roles.** Tuning: regression only. Dev (`dataset/dev.json`, blind-written, 219 items): where
  every change is judged, by kind, never item by item; scored as often as needed. Held-out: scored once more, on the
  phone, only with changes that held on dev.
- **The emulator.** An arm64 AVD on the X10 Pro SSD runs the debug build with E2B's CPU bundle: the tuning split in
  20 min, the dev set in 25, first words 6 s. Parity with the phone: same outcome on 197 of 202, 80% passage overlap,
  grounded 102 vs 101; the keyword rewrite differs on about half the questions (CPU vs GPU numerics). Deltas come
  from the emulator; the pass-or-stop number from the phone. `~/.cache/granger/emu-stage/start-emulator.sh`,
  `load-library.sh`; `ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-5580`; `eval.py run … --passages N` exists now.
- **Grading** is a Fable agent per run (about 5 min, ~350k tokens for 190 replies); the blind grader's by-kind report
  is the model for reading the held-out set without seeing it.
- **Noise** is about ±4 answered items per set; a lever has to clear it on dev before it's believed.

## 6. The decision ahead (Task 11)

Scoring 1 of 2 failed (67% / 85%). Dev says 56% / 84% with 46% of the misses on the model. If the remaining retrieval
levers and a self-check pass don't lift dev by five points or more, 80% on E2B needs the LoRA or a different model,
and the fail path applies: Ask as a Preview with these numbers on record (amend spec §8 and §11), or off in release
until the LoRA path clears. Neal's call; a fresh brainstorming session starts from here.
