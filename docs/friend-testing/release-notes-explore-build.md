# Release notes: the Explore build (draft, Neal approves)

The first build since 1309 (closed track). Main from `511dc010` on; version code at build time. In it: Explore in place
of Browse (Plan EX), the topic page in front of each article (LL2), reference links and quotes in the Classics (LL1),
books by kind with African Storybook and book packs as collections (KB2), adult topics and pictures kept out of
suggestions (roadmap, Carried forward from EX), and Kiwix search finding book packs.

## Play "What's new"

English (≤500; 476):
> Explore replaces Browse: wander your library by subject, room and era, with picture spreads for big topics. Articles
> now open at a topic page first: the gist, people and events of its time, related pages and quotes from your books;
> Read the full article is one tap. The Classics link the people and places they mention. Get books is sorted by kind,
> with African Storybook sets for children, and text PDFs can be read as ebooks. Adult topics stay out of suggestions
> (Settings).

Español (es-419, ≤500; 474). In the Spanish app Browse was already "Explorar", so it's "se renueva", not "reemplaza":
> Explorar se renueva: recorre tu biblioteca por tema, sala y época, con láminas para los grandes temas. Los artículos
> abren primero en una página de tema: lo esencial, personas y sucesos de su época, páginas afines y citas de tus
> libros. Los Clásicos enlazan a las personas y lugares que mencionan. Conseguir libros se ordena por tipo, con African
> Storybook para niños, y los PDF de texto se leen como libros. Los temas para adultos quedan fuera de las sugerencias
> (Ajustes).

## For testers to know (tester note, both languages, when it's revised)

- Updating from 1309 downloads each Wikipedia's index file with its pack (most 2–17 MB; Spanish "all" mini 287 MB,
  Top 1M 142 MB), on Wi-Fi unless that setting is off. Explore and the topic pages need it.
- Settings → Explore → "Keep adult topics out of suggestions" is on; search and opened articles are never filtered.
- "Open articles at" (the reader's Aa sheet) takes you straight to the article instead of the topic page.
