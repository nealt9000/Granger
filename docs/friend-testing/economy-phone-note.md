# Granger on a 4 GB phone: a note for field testers

This goes with the round 1 tester note (`round-1-tester-note.md`). It's for testers whose phones have about 4 GB of memory and 64 GB of storage, the kind of phone Granger most needs to work well on. Your week of real use tells us more than any lab test.

## What's different on your phone

- **Ask, the librarian, is hidden.** It needs 6 GB of memory. Everything else works: search, the encyclopedias, books, maps, guides, Listen, music and audiobooks.
- **Listen uses your phone's own voice.** The Granger voices (the owl) are under Settings → Voice with a note that they may stop partway on this phone. Please try one once and tell us what happened (see "Read aloud" below).
- If your phone has no English voice yet: Android Settings → System → Languages & input → Text-to-speech output → Speech Services by Google → Install voice data → English. Check for Swahili there too, and tell us if it's offered.

## Setting up

1. On **Pick a start**, choose **Efficient start**. With pictures it's about 17 GB; with **Save space** on (no pictures) about 9 GB. Granger turns Save space on by itself when pictures would leave you under 10 GB free. Turn it off if you'd rather have the pictures and have the room.
2. **Your region:** Tanzania's map (0.6 GB) should already be picked. If it suggests another country, please tell us.
3. **If you have a microSD card**, Review offers it under **Store on**, and picks it by itself when it has more room than the phone. A big card is the best place for a growing library.
4. Add what your life needs from **Get more**: medicine, repair, farming, cooking, science. **Get more → Kiwix → All languages** has Swahili Wikipedia (750 MB, or 260 MB without pictures), Swahili Wiktionary, and PhET science simulations in Swahili.

## Getting packs without much data

- **Downloads wait for Wi-Fi.** If you're on a phone's hotspot or a pocket router, Android may call that Wi-Fi "metered" and Granger will wait. Downloads says so and has a **Wi-Fi settings** button. There, open the network and set it to unmetered, or turn off "Download on Wi-Fi only" in Granger's Settings.
- A download that drops picks up where it stopped, even after many drops.
- **From a computer or another phone:** download the `.zim` file once (from download.kiwix.org on a computer in town, or copy it from a friend), put it on the SD card or a USB stick, then **Browse → ⋮ → Add a pack from a file** and pick it (it's also under **Get packs** while Browse is empty). Granger copies it into your library with no internet. Downloads shows the copy's progress. A pack Granger knows is checked against its catalog, so a damaged copy is caught.

## What to try over a week

1. Search for things you'd really look up: a medicine, a repair, a word, a place.
2. Read a WikiMed article and an iFixit guide with airplane mode on.
3. **Read aloud:** open a Granger Classics book, tap Listen, lock the screen for 10 minutes. Then the same with a Wikipedia article open and music paused. Note whether it stops, stutters or Granger restarts, with the phone's voice and once with a Granger voice.
4. Let a big download drop (walk out of Wi-Fi range) and see it resume.
5. Add one pack from a file.
6. If you have an SD card: download to it, restart the phone, check the packs still open. Then, with Granger closed, take the card out. Granger should show the packs as missing, not crash. Put it back and they should open again.

## What to send (Settings → Send feedback)

- Free space before and after setting up, and where the library went (phone or card).
- Anything that restarted, froze or was slow, and what you were doing.
- Roughly how much battery Granger used on a normal day.
- What you added to your library and why, and what you looked for and didn't find.

Your phone model and Android version are included automatically. Nothing is sent until you press send.
