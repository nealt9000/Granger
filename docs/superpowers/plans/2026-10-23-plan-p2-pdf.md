# Plan P2: The PDF pass — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** PDFs open in the e-book reader, at their last page, from three places: My books (imported), archive.org (PDF-only items, and the PDF beside an OCR text), and Kiwix packs (a tapped PDF link). The text layer of a book PDF is indexed, so Search and Research find it and open at the page. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-pdf-design.md` (decided 2026-10-23, §9).

**Out of this plan** (spec §2, §7): OCR; highlights, notes, the selection menu and in-book search on PDF pages; read-aloud of PDFs; indexing pack PDFs; dense vectors for PDFs; joining an archive.org text and PDF into one book.

**Architecture (what changes):**
- **Build:** JitPack as a third repository, limited to `com.github.marain87`; `readium-adapter-pdfium` 3.4.0 in `:app`; `pdfbox-android` 2.0.27.0 in `:core:books`.
- **`:core:books`:** `PdfInspector` (metadata, encryption, damage) and `PdfText` (deterministic per-page chunks, `href = "page/<n>"`), and `BookMeta`, the format-neutral result `EpubInspector` also returns. `PdfFixtures` in test fixtures builds the test PDFs with PdfBox at test time, so no binaries are committed.

## Decisions (spec §9 and this plan)

1. **PDFium through Readium**, with JitPack restricted to the `com.github.marain87` group.
2. **Text by PdfBox-Android**, rendering by PDFium, and covers by platform `PdfRenderer`. Three readers of one file is fine: each is used for one job, and PdfBox is never on the reading path.
3. **Chunk keys:** `href = "page/<n>"` (1-based), `ordinal` within the page, `progression` within the page's text. A chunk never spans pages.
4. **Searchable** is decided at indexing: fewer than 200 characters across the first 20 pages → `searchable = 0`. Until a PDF is indexed it counts as searchable (the default), so it isn't labelled wrongly before the worker runs.
5. **Pack documents are hidden `Book` rows** (`origin = PACK`), so places, bookmarks, the rail, History and launch need no new id scheme.
6. **The 100 MB prompt** has "Don't ask again", backed by `DownloadPrefs.askOverBytes` (Boolean, default true), shown beside "Download on Wi-Fi only".
7. **Text and PDF of one item are two books**: the PDF's `BookIds.of(ARCHIVE_ORG, "<id>#pdf")` and title "<title> (PDF)". The text keeps `BookIds.of(ARCHIVE_ORG, "<id>")`, so existing downloads are unchanged.
